Patient Information Management System

An NDR-linked platform designed to support continuity of ART services across facilities and reduce duplicate enrolment.

MY ROLEData, programme requirements and monitoring

Coordinated the minimum service dataset and provided data-system support for implementation.

Continuity of care across facilities

CDC proposed PIMS in 2022 as an extension of biometric integration and record deduplication. The system uses fingerprint matching to help authorised staff identify an existing treatment record and support patients who need services away from their primary treatment facility.

From requirements to rollout

I helped strengthen biometric coverage and the deduplicated data foundation, coordinated the minimum dataset for decentralised service delivery and contributed HIV programme and data expertise to implementation. At the April 2024 NAWS–PIMS stakeholder event, government and PHIS3 reviewed the minimum dataset and rollout priorities.

In 2025, more than 2,000 HIV treatment facilities received rollout support through direct visits, implementing-partner assistance and virtual sessions. I developed Power BI monitoring for system availability and processing time.
