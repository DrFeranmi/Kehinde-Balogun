Implemented surveillance workflows that use routine data to support national and subnational review.

National HIV surveillance

From October 2021, I led technical implementation support for the scale-up of HIV mortality surveillance and verbal autopsy, recent-infection surveillance, and case-based surveillance. The work included protocol and tool adaptation, training, monitoring, dashboards, analysis and evidence production for NASCP.

For case-based surveillance, I co-developed metrics with CDC and national stakeholders and led their translation into functional requirements, validation, dashboard development, training and rollout of the NDR module. I led production of a national factsheet for NASCP. I also led development of the integrated HIV surveillance guideline and the implementation of HIV drug-resistance early warning indicators using routine NDR data.

From evidence to programme review

A government-led review of unexpectedly high AIDS-related mortality identified default assumptions in the verbal-autopsy analysis. My team supported the review and reprocessing. Recent-infection analysis also helped raise a hypothesis about repeat ART enrolment that informed further verification. In 2026, Akwa Ibom survey findings prompted renewed discussion about early warning indicator monitoring.

Multi-disease reporting and interoperability

Across 18 CDC-supported states, I led data-system support for Mpox, COVID-19 and Lassa fever through NDRLite, Power BI, workflow automation and training. COVID-19 support covered case surveillance and vaccination monitoring. My team defined programme requirements and indicators for operational NDR–DHIS2 and NDR–SORMAS exchanges, while Health Informatics built the interfaces. The integrations were handed over to NASCP and NCDC respectively.
