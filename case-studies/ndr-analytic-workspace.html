NATIONAL ANALYTICS PRODUCT · 2021–2025

NDR Analytic Workspace

Turning Nigeria’s national HIV repository into an accessible, reusable environment for programme analysis and decision-making.

MY ROLEConcept author and senior technical & implementation leader

From the initial 2021 concept through stakeholder development, launch and government-led capacity transfer.

The challenge

Nigeria’s National Data Repository contained a rich national HIV dataset, but recurring analysis still depended heavily on specialist support and fragmented workflows. Government, implementing partners and donors needed a governed workspace that made agreed indicators, reusable analysis and downloadable outputs accessible to authorised users.

From concept to institutional product

I authored the initial NAWS concept in 2021 and provided sustained senior leadership as it developed into a collaborative institutional product involving NASCP, PHIS3, donors, implementing partners and other national stakeholders.

2021

Initial concept

Defined the problem, objectives, proposed architecture, analytic themes and implementation pathway.

2022–2023

Requirements and refinement

Stakeholders shaped requirements, evaluated the workspace and incorporated feedback.

APRIL 2024

National launch

Approximately 85 government, implementer, donor and national-level stakeholders participated, with training integrated.

OCTOBER–NOVEMBER 2025

National capacity transfer

A government training-of-trainers preceded Northern and Southern cohorts led principally by NASCP facilitators.

Selected verified events—not an exhaustive history of every workshop, resource or contribution.

Leadership through government ownership

My strongest contribution was product and programme leadership, not being the most visible classroom facilitator. I presented the platform’s history during the government training and supported NASCP staff to lead the regional cohorts—placing public delivery with the institution responsible for governance and long-term use.

~85launch participants
140+regional training attendances
36 + FCTnational stakeholder reach

What this demonstrates

  • Problem framing and concept development for a national digital-health product.
  • Multi-year coordination across government, donors and implementers.
  • Translation of complex national data into reusable analytical workflows.
  • Capacity transfer centred on government ownership and sustainability.

Attribution: NAWS is an institutional, collaborative product under NASCP governance leadership, with PHIS3 technical support and stakeholder contributions. This narrative describes my specific concept, technical and implementation responsibilities.
